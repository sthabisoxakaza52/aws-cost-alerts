Event-Driven Cost Monitoring Architecture

This serverless pattern evaluates spend at zero baseline cost and distributes alerts instantly across email and Slack.

AWS Budgets

Monitors monthly cloud spend against limits and triggers alarms at 50%, 80%, 100%, and forecast.

Amazon SNS Topic

aws-cost-alert-topic fans out events to confirmed email subscribers and Lambda forwarder.

AWS Lambda

aws-cost-alert-slack-forwarder formats event payloads and executes HTTPS webhook to Slack.

Slack Channel

Immediate team visibility in #alerts with interactive spend badges and action links.
